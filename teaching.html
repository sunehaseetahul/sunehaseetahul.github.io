Teaching

I have taught development, human rights, statistics and applied econometrics in Australia, France and Chile.

Unit design and coordination

  • Human rights in development; Human rights and international developmentUnit coordinator and lecturer. Designed and delivered units centred on the Sustainable Development Goals, examining development policy and practice through a multidisciplinary lens. University of Sydney, Department of Political Economy, 2019 to 2020
  • Research methods in development economicsUnit coordinator and lecturer, postgraduate. Research design and empirical methods, using published studies to introduce experimental and quasi-experimental designs. University of Bordeaux, 2016 to 2017
  • Statistical analysisUnit coordinator and lecturer, postgraduate. Applied statistics and data analysis in Stata built around a single real-world case study, from data cleaning to estimating a causal model. University of Bordeaux, 2016 to 2017

Tutoring

  • Microeconomics; descriptive statistics; statistical analysis in StataWeekly tutorials and computer lab sessions, undergraduate. University of Bordeaux, 2013 to 2019

Teaching innovation

  • Interdisciplinary Impact unitAcademic and industry partner (2025) and workshop lead (2021), mentoring students to co-design policy projects through experiential learning. University of Sydney
  • Global Classroom on Development PolicyAcademic lead. Designed and led a cross-institutional global classroom with the University of Toronto and the Tata Institute of Social Sciences, with delivery adapted to COVID-19, 2019
