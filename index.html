Suneha Seetahul

Senior Research Fellow, Australian Centre for Gender Equality and Inclusion at Work
University of Sydney Business School

I am an applied microeconomist working mainly on gender inequality in labour markets, health and development. In my research, I investigate how gender inequality, income inequality and living conditions shape outcomes at work and in health, from job quality and pay to obesity, malnutrition and food security. Linking survey, administrative and geospatial data allows me to measure these inequalities precisely and to understand the gender norms and institutions that produce and sustain them, so that policy can act on the evidence.

At the Australian Centre for Gender Equality and Inclusion at Work, I am the academic and quantitative lead of the Gender Equality @ Work Index, which tracks gender equality across seven dimensions of work in Australia and is used by government to evaluate policy. I am a Chief Investigator on the Working for Women program with the Office for Women and lead researcher on the University of Sydney's Gender Equality Plan project. Alongside my academic work, I collaborate closely with international organisations such as the World Bank and UN Women, through joint research and consulting, including on social protection in Sierra Leone and Indonesia. I hold a PhD in Economics from the University of Bordeaux.

Four connected themes

My research studies inequalities in work, health and wellbeing, particularly gender inequalities: how they arise, what sustains them and how policy can address them.

Gender equality in labour markets and decent work

The Gender Equality @ Work Index, pay, job quality and women's working conditions.

Health, nutrition and wellbeing

Social determinants of obesity and malnutrition, food and nutrition security, and health and safety at work.

Gender norms and behaviour

How parental leave design, social identity and climate shocks shift attitudes, preferences and choices.

Social protection and development

Cash transfers, public works and program targeting for households facing poverty and shocks.

Research for policy

Much of my work is built with or for government and international organisations.

Gender Equality @ Work Index

Launched by the Assistant Minister for Women in 2025, used to evaluate the Building Women's Careers program and cited by the Office for Women. Now extended to state, industry and organisational levels.

World Bank

Social Protection and Jobs Global Practice, Development Impact Evaluation and the Indonesia Country Office, 2021 to 2026. Africa Fellow with the Gender Group, 2021.

Australian Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade

Gender and labour research partner for the Investing in Women initiative, with UN Women, including country reports on COVID-19 and the private sector.

Policy advisory work

Evidence and advice for government and industry partners through the Australian Centre for Gender Equality and Inclusion at Work, including policy papers on women's work in NSW and research leadership for the University of Sydney's Gender Equality Plan.
